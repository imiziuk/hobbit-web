D3D8 replay diff

Replays a .d3dr recording of the original game (d3drec proxy) through the WebGL2 D3D8 layer in web/d3d8/ and compares every recorded back-buffer shot with the replayed back buffer. Serve Hobbit-Decomp/ over HTTP (e.g. python -m http.server 5198 --directory Hobbit-Decomp) and open /d3drec/replay.html, or pass ?log=<url>.
